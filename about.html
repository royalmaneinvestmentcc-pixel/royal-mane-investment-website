About Royal Mane

Building a Namibian innovation and business-development ecosystem.

Based in Oshana Region, Namibia

Royal Mane Investment CC is rooted in the Oshana Region, one of Namibia's most dynamic and populous areas. This strategic location puts us at the heart of Northern Namibia's economic activity and provides unique insights into the region's opportunities and challenges.

Our base in Oshana allows us to:

  • Identify opportunities and challenges specific to Northern Namibia
  • Work directly with entrepreneurs, businesses and communities
  • Understand housing, market, and economic development needs
  • Build practical, locally-informed solutions
  • Connect regional opportunities with national and international partners
