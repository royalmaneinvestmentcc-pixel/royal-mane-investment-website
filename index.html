Explore Our Work Partner With Us

A Namibian venture built around opportunity.

Royal Mane Investment CC is a Namibian-owned innovation and business-development venture based in the Oshana Region.

We work at the intersection of entrepreneurship, technology, research, investment, youth development and strategic partnerships.

Our role is to identify opportunities, develop practical initiatives and connect people, organisations and resources to create economic value.
