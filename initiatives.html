SolveNam

What is SolveNam?

SolveNam is a Royal Mane initiative focused on empowering unemployed and emerging Namibian innovators to develop technology-based solutions to national problems.

We identify real challenges facing Namibia—in housing, energy, agriculture, health, education, employment and other sectors—and connect innovators, entrepreneurs and technologists to develop practical solutions.

What We Provide

  • Mentorship: Access to experienced innovators, entrepreneurs and technologists
  • Technical Support: Guidance on technology development and implementation
  • Funding: Grant support for prototype development and pilot testing
  • Networking: Connection to investors, partners, and potential customers
  • Training: Skills development in business, technology, and entrepreneurship
  • Platform: Showcase your solution to government, corporates, and investors
